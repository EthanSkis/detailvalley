Why Valley County books Detail Valley

Detail Valley is a mobile car detailing service built around one simple idea: a spotless car shouldn't cost you a Saturday. We bring all our own gear and pro-grade products straight to your home or workplace, so you get a showroom finish without ever leaving your driveway.

Every detail is done by hand. From a quick Maintenance Wash to a full interior-and-exterior detail or our Showroom clay-and-wax finish, we treat each vehicle like it's our own — wheels, glass, seats, carpets, and trim all included. No rushed, assembly-line work and no surprise fees on the day.

We're proud to serve Valley County, Idaho and the surrounding communities, including McCall, Cascade, and Donnelly. Compare our detailing packages, see the areas we serve in Valley County, or book your detail online in under a minute.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Anything else? Call or text (208) 315-1420.

Do I need to provide anything?

Just access to an outdoor water tap and a power outlet near where the car is parked. We bring everything else — hoses, cords, machines, products and towels.

How long does a detail take?

A Maintenance Wash runs about 1½–2 hours. An Interior Detail takes 3½–4 hours, a Full Detail around 6, and a Showroom finish 6½–7½. You don't need to stay — most customers just hand us the keys.

Do I pay a deposit to book?

No. You book, we do the work, you look the car over, and then you pay — cash, check, cards, Venmo, PayPal or Cash App.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Valley County — McCall, Cascade, Donnelly, Lake Fork, New Meadows and everywhere in between. See the full list on our service areas page.

What if the weather turns bad?

If the forecast won't cooperate, we'll text you and move your booking to the next slot that works — no charge to reschedule. A garage or carport also works great in any season.

Is Showroom the same as paint correction?

No — Showroom is a clay-and-wax finish: decontaminated, protected and glossed. Machine paint correction is a different, multi-day service.

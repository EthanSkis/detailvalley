Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

The short version

  • We only collect what we need to book and do your detail: name, phone, address and vehicle details.
  • We never sell your information or share it for anyone else's marketing.
  • Our website has no ad trackers or analytics cookies.
  • Want your information deleted? Text or email us and we'll do it.

This policy explains what information Detail Valley collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to detailvalley.com and to bookings made by text, phone, email or social media.

1. Information we collect

When you book or contact us, you give us:

  • Your name and phone number
  • The address and town where your vehicle will be
  • Your vehicle size or make and model, the package and add-ons you pick, and your appointment time
  • Whether a water tap and power outlet are available
  • Anything else you choose to tell us in a text, call, email or social media message, including photos of your vehicle

When we do the job, we may take before and after photos of your vehicle.

When you pay, payment is handled by the service you choose (your card processor, Venmo, PayPal or Cash App). We don't store your card number. We keep a simple record of what was paid and how.

When you visit our website, our hosting provider and the services that deliver our fonts and code automatically receive basic technical information like your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for. This is standard for any website. We don't use analytics, advertising cookies, tracking pixels or retargeting.

2. How we use it

  • To schedule, confirm and show up for your appointment
  • To text or call you about your booking: confirmations, reminders, arrival times, weather changes and follow-ups
  • To price the job and take payment
  • To fix anything you're not happy with and answer your questions
  • To keep basic business and tax records
  • To share before and after photos of our work, without license plates, faces, house numbers or other identifying details (you can opt out, see below)

3. Text messages

If you give us your phone number, we'll text you about your appointment. We don't send marketing texts unless you've asked for them. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help.

We do not sell, rent or share your phone number or text messaging consent with any third party for their marketing purposes.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We only share it with the services we use to run the business, and only as much as they need:

  • Supabase, which stores booking records for our online booking system
  • Our email provider, which sends us a notice when a new booking comes in
  • Our phone carrier, for calls and texts
  • Payment services (card processor, Venmo, PayPal, Cash App) when you pay us. Their own privacy policies apply to what they collect.
  • Website services: GitHub Pages (hosting), Google Fonts (fonts) and jsDelivr (code library), which receive the basic technical information described above

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.

5. How long we keep it

We keep booking records as long as they're useful for serving you again and for our business and tax records, generally no more than a few years. If you ask us to delete your information, we will, except for anything we're legally required to keep.

6. Your choices

  • See, correct or delete your information: text or email us and we'll take care of it.
  • Stop texts: reply STOP to any message from us.
  • Photos: tell us if you don't want photos of your vehicle posted, and we'll leave them out or take them down.

7. Security

Booking data is stored in a database that only we can sign in to, and the website runs over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). No system is perfect, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and only keep what we need.

8. Children

Our services are for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. If we make a big change in how we use your information, we'll post it here before it takes effect.

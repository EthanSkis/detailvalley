These terms cover any detailing service you book with Detail Valley, whether you book on detailvalley.com, by text, by phone, by email or through social media. By booking a service you agree to these terms. If you have a question about any of this, call or text (208) 315-1420 before you book.

1. Who we are

Detail Valley is a mobile car detailing business based in Valley County, Idaho. We come to your home, cabin, rental or workplace in McCall, Cascade, Donnelly, Lake Fork, New Meadows and the surrounding area.

2. Booking and confirmation

When you book online, you pick a package, your vehicle size, any add-ons and a time. Your booking is a request until we confirm it, usually by text to the phone number you gave us. If we can't make that time or can't reach your location, we'll tell you and offer another option.

Please give us accurate information: your name, a phone number that can receive texts, the address where the vehicle will be, and whether a water tap and power outlet are available.

3. Prices and payment

  • Prices on the website are based on the package, vehicle size and add-ons you choose. The total shown when you book is what we expect to charge.
  • If the vehicle's condition is very different from a normal job (for example heavy pet hair, mold, biohazards like vomit or bodily fluids, or extreme mud or sand), we'll tell you what extra time or cost is involved before we do the extra work. You can say no and we'll do the original package as best we can.
  • No deposit is required. Payment is due when the job is finished. We accept cash, check, credit and debit cards, Venmo, PayPal and Cash App.

4. Cancelling and rescheduling

Life happens. If you need to cancel or move your appointment, please text or call us as early as you can, ideally at least 24 hours ahead, so we can offer the slot to someone else. We don't charge a cancellation fee. If you repeatedly cancel at the last minute or aren't there when we arrive and we can't reach you, we may decline future bookings.

Weather: We work outdoors. If rain, snow, wind or cold makes it unsafe or impossible to do a good job, we'll text you and move your booking to the next slot that works, at no charge. A garage or carport lets us work in most weather.

5. What we need from you

  • Access to an outdoor water tap and a standard power outlet near where the vehicle is parked, unless we've agreed otherwise ahead of time.
  • A safe, reasonably level place to work, and permission to work there (for example from your HOA, landlord or property manager).
  • The keys, or a way to get them, at the appointment time.
  • Please remove valuables, cash, documents, child seats you want left in place, and anything fragile before we start. We aren't responsible for personal items left in the vehicle.
  • Let us know about anything we should be careful with, like aftermarket parts, loose trim, fresh paint, or known electrical issues.

6. What to expect from the work

We do every job carefully and by hand. That said, some things can't be fully fixed by detailing, including permanent stains, sun or UV fading, burns, tears, deep scratches, rock chips, etched water spots, and damage that was already there. Our Showroom package is a clay-and-wax finish, not machine paint correction, so it won't remove scratches or swirl marks.

Before we start, we'll walk around the vehicle and may take photos to note any existing damage.

7. Satisfaction

Look the car over before you pay. If something isn't right, tell us while we're there and we'll fix it on the spot. If you notice something after we've left, text us within 48 hours with a photo and we'll come back and make it right at no charge.

8. Damage and liability

If we damage your vehicle or property through our own mistake, tell us as soon as possible, ideally within 48 hours, and we'll work with you to repair it or cover the reasonable cost of repair.

We're not responsible for pre-existing damage or wear, damage caused by defects in the vehicle (like failing clear coat, loose trim, or electrical problems), items left in the vehicle, or issues caused by information you didn't share with us. To the fullest extent allowed by Idaho law, our total liability for any claim related to a service is limited to the cost of repair or the amount you paid for that service, whichever is greater, and we aren't liable for indirect losses like loss of use or rental cars. Nothing in these terms limits any rights you have that can't be waived under the law.

9. Photos

We like to take before and after photos of our work and may share them on our website and social media. We don't show license plates, faces, house numbers or other identifying details. If you'd rather we not post photos of your vehicle, just tell us and we won't.

10. Text messages

By booking and giving us your phone number, you agree that we can text you about your appointment: confirmations, reminders, arrival times, weather changes and follow-ups. We don't send marketing texts without asking first. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. See our privacy policy for how we handle your information.

If something on the site is wrong, we'll let you know before the job and you can cancel without any charge.

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Idaho. If we ever have a dispute, we'd much rather sort it out by talking, so please reach out first. If we can't resolve it, it will be handled in the courts of Valley County, Idaho, including small claims court where it applies.

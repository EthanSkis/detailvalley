Towns we serve

Detail Valley is a fully mobile detailing service, so your driveway is our shop. We cover McCall, Cascade, Donnelly and the smaller communities in between, bringing the same hand-washed, interior-and-exterior detail to every stop. If you're nearby but don't see your town listed, start a booking or text us and we'll let you know if we can reach you.

McCall, ID

Lake life is hard on a vehicle — sand, sunscreen, pine sap and dust off the forest roads. We bring full interior and exterior detailing right to your home or rental in McCall so your car is ready for the next trip up to Payette Lake.

Cascade, ID

From boat-launch grime to gravel-road dust, Cascade drives leave a mark. Our mobile detail covers a deep interior clean, wheels, glass and a protective wax finish without you ever leaving the house.

Donnelly, ID

Whether it's a daily commuter or a weekend truck, we come to Donnelly with everything needed for a complete detail — hand wash, vacuum, clay bar and wax — done in your own driveway.

Lake Fork & nearby

Tucked between McCall and Donnelly, Lake Fork is right on our route. We handle everything from a quick Maintenance Wash to a full Showroom clay-and-wax detail for homes and cabins across the area.

New Meadows, ID

Just west of the valley, New Meadows is part of our regular service area. We bring mobile detailing to your door so your vehicle looks its best year-round, mud season included.
